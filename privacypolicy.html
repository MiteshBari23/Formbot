# FormBot – Google Forms Auto-Filler Privacy Policy **Last updated:** November 2, 2026 ## 1. Introduction FormBot – Google Forms Auto-Filler ("FormBot", "the Extension") is a browser extension designed to help users automate repetitive Google Forms tasks through reusable answer templates, form filling, and scheduled submissions. This Privacy Policy explains how FormBot handles user information. ## 2. Information We Access FormBot may access the following information when users use its features: - Google Forms question labels and form structure. - User-provided answers and saved answer templates. - Form URLs and scheduling preferences. - Extension settings and task execution status. ## 3. Data Storage FormBot stores saved answer templates, form configurations, schedules, and settings locally in the user's browser using browser storage APIs. This information remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to external servers by FormBot. ## 4. Data Collection and Sharing FormBot does not operate servers that collect or store users' form responses. FormBot does not sell, share, or transfer users' personal information to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Form submissions are sent directly to the relevant Google Forms service when users initiate or configure submission functionality. ## 5. Permissions FormBot uses browser permissions only to provide its core functionality: - **activeTab:** Access the active form when requested by the user. - **alarms:** Trigger scheduled automation. - **host permissions:** Interact with supported Google Forms pages. - **notifications:** Display task status and submission notifications. - **scripting:** Extract form fields and fill responses. - **storage:** Save templates, schedules, and settings locally. - **tabs:** Identify relevant form tabs for automation. ## 6. User Control Users can review, modify, or delete their saved templates and schedules through the Extension's interface. Users can disable scheduled automation or uninstall the Extension to stop using its functionality. ## 7. Security FormBot is designed to keep user-provided answers and settings stored locally in the browser. Users should avoid saving sensitive information in templates unless they understand the risks associated with storing information in their browser. ## 8. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Extension's functionality changes. The latest version will be available at the published Privacy Policy URL.
